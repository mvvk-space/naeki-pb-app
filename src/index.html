About Naeki · 苗木

The art & craft of onigiri — No.1.

You've seen the black kiosk with the warm glow by the gates. Ten years of one simple promise: a quick comfort stop for people with places to be.

A deliciously perfect fit for your on-the-go lifestyle

Founded in 2013, Naeki began as a small sushi shop at CRC Tower, All Seasons Place — right in the heart of Bangkok. The vision was a “quick comfort stop”: fresh-made onigiri at fair prices, served fast, for people racing through their day.

The mission hasn't moved: be Thailand's go-to onigiri brand, built on fresh, high-quality ingredients, quick service, and preservative-free recipes. Today the kiosks follow the rhythm of modern life — especially along Bangkok's BTS lines — so a satisfying, value-packed bite is never more than a gate away.

Frequently asked questions

We've compiled the questions we hear most here. For anything else, reach out via LINE OA (@naekisushi) — we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Why Naeki's?

We are a Japanese Grab & Go restaurant offering quality meals in a Healthy Quick Service Restaurant (HQSR) format. Our focus is on freshness every day, fast service, and convenience — perfect for busy urban lifestyles where health still matters. With many branches located near you, we're here to be your go-to spot for a meal that's quick, delicious and well-balanced. Next time you think of the perfect meal for your busy day — think of us!

Do you offer catering services?

Currently, Naeki does not offer full-service catering. However, customers can place bulk orders — such as bento sets or sushi trays — for meetings, events or staff giveaways. For more details, contact your nearest Naeki branch, or reach out via Facebook (Naeki GO!) or LINE OA (@naekisushi).

Is your sushi made fresh to order or prepared in advance?

Our sushi is freshly made every day. As a Healthy Quick Service Restaurant, products are prepared through a standardized process at a central kitchen to ensure consistent quality, freshness and food safety. We focus on speed and convenience — the key strengths of both Naeki Sushi and Naeki GO!.

What payment methods do you accept?

We offer a variety of payment options for your convenience:

  • Cash
  • Credit / debit cards
  • QR code payment via mobile banking apps
  • PromptPay
  • Popular e-wallets such as TrueMoney Wallet
I'm interested in franchising — what should I do?

Interested in becoming a Naeki GO! franchise partner? Contact the team via the Naeki GO! Facebook page or LINE OA (@naekisushi) for the franchise application process, initial investment details, benefits and training programs, and full support for store setup and operations. Naeki aims to expand to 50 branches, and they're looking for passionate partners who share a love of Japanese food.
